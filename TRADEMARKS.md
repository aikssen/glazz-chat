# Trademarks and Naming

The Glazz source code is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](./LICENSE). That
license covers the code. It does not grant rights to the project's identity.

## Not covered by the code license

- the name **Glazz**;
- the Glazz logo and icon;
- the Glazz visual identity, including its color system and design assets under
  `docs/design/`;
- any future commercial names derived from Glazz.

All rights to those remain with Ever Cifuentes (aikssen).

## What you may do

- Use, modify, and redistribute the code under Apache-2.0.
- Run your own instance, including a modified one.
- State factually that your work is based on, derived from, or compatible with
  Glazz.
- Reference the name in documentation, articles, and talks that discuss the project.

## What requires permission

- Naming your product, service, or fork **Glazz**, or a name likely to be confused
  with it.
- Using the logo or visual identity to brand your own distribution.
- Implying that your deployment is the official Glazz service, or that it is
  endorsed by or affiliated with the project owner.

If you distribute a modified version, give it your own name and make clear that it
is not the official project.

## Design assets

Mockups and design explorations under `docs/design/` are illustrative artifacts of
the design process. Some were produced with generative image tooling, and their
provenance is recorded in the design documentation. They are not licensed for reuse
as product branding.
